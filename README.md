What those lists *are* good for is the roster of names, and for the one distinction most of them bury: whether a tool is built to send more email or to qualify people who already raised a hand.

That distinction decides what you pay, what you get, and whether the free tier you're eyeing is actually enough to run anything.

## The split that matters more than any feature grid

Strip away the category labels and AI SDR tools fall into two groups.

**Autonomous outbound senders.** 11x (Alice), Artisan (Ava), and AiSDR find prospects, write the first touch, run the sequence, and handle replies. The pitch is volume without headcount. The problem is that volume stopped being scarce. Salesmotion's 2026 writeup cites industry data showing per-rep monthly outbound jumped from roughly 1,150 emails to 7,400 after AI adoption, while raw reply rates fell from 4.7% to 2.9% — and about one in six emails now never reaches an inbox at all. Buyers aren't reading six times more email; they're filtering it. Gartner's forecast that more than 40% of agentic AI projects get cancelled by the end of 2027 describes exactly this failure mode: activity without business value.

**Inbound and messaging qualifiers.** Qualified's Piper works website visitors. Knock AI works Slack, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. Conversica and Drift sit in the same neighbourhood. These agents don't manufacture demand — they answer it, qualify it, and put it on a calendar. Most of the "best AI SDR" roundups treat this as a footnote, which is odd, because inbound is where the buying intent already exists.

And there's a third, less crowded spot: the text conversations sitting inside the CRM you already pay for. WhatsApp replies, Instagram DMs routed through your inbox, SMS, live chat, email threads. That's the lane CloseBot occupies.

Whoever quotes you a headline price is usually quoting you one of the three.

Two caveats on that table. The layer most buyers underestimate is the one they never see on a pricing page: deliverability. Sending 2,000 cold emails a day safely means 40–60 warmed inboxes across separate domains. Some of these tools include that. Others quietly assume you'll spend another $200–$600 a month on it.

## The benchmark data is the most useful thing CloseBot has published

Most AI booking advice cites a handful of accounts in one niche. In its 1.1-million-appointment analysis, CloseBot published the numbers behind the claims — including the parts that undercut its own charts.

The findings that change how you'd set up any AI setter, not just this one:

- **Just over half of bookings happen outside 9-to-5**, measured in the lead's local time. About a third land at 5pm or later. Roughly 11% happen between midnight and 6am — a window that produces about as many appointments as the entire day of Saturday.
- **Monday through Wednesday carry 50.8% of bookings**, with Monday highest at 17.5% and Sunday lowest at 10%.
- **Sunday is the cheapest day to convert.** A Friday booking takes about 29% more messages than a Sunday one, and weekends overall close with 14% fewer messages than weekdays. Fewer leads reply on a Sunday, but the ones who do are there to book.
- **Expect roughly 132 messages per booking**, counting inbound messages and leads who never respond. Agencies that model on converting leads only will underquote clients badly.
- **One lead booked 355 days after first contact**, from a follow-up sequence that never expired. Default 30-day windows cut off long before that tail.
- **About 12% of bookings involve more than one channel.** Facebook, Instagram, and live chat together hold just 4.2% of last-touch credit, so a last-touch report makes channels that start conversations look worthless.

CloseBot states its own caveats: the channel data skews toward its SMS-heavy customer base, some figures are derived rather than directly measured, and per-conversation conversion rates aren't proven by volume charts. That's disclosed honestly, which is more than most benchmark posts manage — but it's still one vendor's platform data, not an independent study. Use it to set follow-up windows and staffing hours, not as a promise about your results.

## Where CloseBot is the wrong choice

- **It lives inside a CRM.** Channels come from your CRM, not from CloseBot. A third-party review makes the point bluntly: CloseBot has no standalone Instagram or WhatsApp connection of its own, so a solo operator whose entire pipeline is DMs would be buying a CRM plus an agent. That's two products instead of one.
- **No voice agent.** G2's pros-and-cons summary for CloseBot lists ease of use and fast setup on the positive side, and limited bot functionality — including irrelevant answers and no voice interaction — on the negative side. If your motion needs an AI to answer the phone, 11x's Jordan agent or a voice-first vendor covers that; CloseBot does text.
- **Reddit isn't unanimous.** Threads in r/gohighlevel comparing CloseBot to GHL's native Conversation AI range from "generally better than GHL's Conversation AI" to one commenter calling it unreliable. Both views are in the wild. The free plan is how you decide which one matches your setup.
- **The free plan caps out fast.** 100 messages a month, one agent, one seat, 1 MB of knowledge storage. Usable for a live test. Not usable for a real pipeline.

## Choosing without another listicle

Work backwards from the problem:

- **Pipeline is empty and your TAM is enormous.** An autonomous sender (11x, Artisan, AiSDR) with hard volume caps and separated sending domains. Budget 1.5x to 2x the advertised price.
- **Reps write generic outreach because research eats the day.** An augmentation tool that prepares the signal and the draft keeps the human in the loop.
- **Your website leaks demand nobody answers in time.** A conversion agent — Qualified, Knock AI, or a CRM-side agent like CloseBot.
- **Leads already message you, and nobody replies after hours.** Given that more than half of bookings land outside business hours, this is the cheapest gap to close, and it's the one CloseBot is priced for: $0 to test, $64/mo at the entry business tier, $397/mo if you're reselling to clients.
